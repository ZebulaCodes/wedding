The Resort

There will be two resorts that you can stay at in the same location: an adults-only (Barceló Maya Riviera) and a family-friendly (Maya Palace). They are located on the Caribbean coast in Xpu-Ha, between Playa del Carmen and Tulum. It's part of the larger Barceló Maya Grand Resort.

🌴

Adults Only Resort

All guests must be 18 or older. Expect a calm, grown-up vibe with access to the wider Barceló Maya complex.

🌴

Family-Friendly Resort

Perfect for families with children. Enjoy a variety of activities and amenities designed for all ages.

🍹

All-Inclusive

Meals, snacks, and drinks are included — buffets, à la carte restaurants, pools, and beach bars.

✈️

Getting There

Fly into Cancún (CUN). The resort is about 80 km south — roughly 1 hr 15 min by car. Ask Mandy about transfers.

🔑

Check-In / Out

Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability.
